SAMQUANT PHASE 6 - VERY EASY EXPLANATION

WHAT IS PHASE 6?

Phase 6 is the screen of SamQuant.

Earlier phases did the work in the background:
- get market data
- create strategy signals
- simulate trades
- calculate returns and risk

Phase 6 puts all those results into one interactive dashboard.


WHAT CAN YOU CHOOSE?

- Demo data or Yahoo Finance data
- Stock symbols, such as AAPL, MSFT, and NVDA
- Moving average, mean reversion, or momentum strategy
- Starting cash
- Trading commission
- Fixed trading fee
- Slippage
- Risk-free rate


WHAT DOES SAMQUANT DO AFTER THAT?

1. It gets and checks the market data.
2. The selected strategy creates target weights.
3. The backtester trades at the next market open.
4. It tracks cash, shares, fees, and portfolio value.
5. Phase 5 calculates return and risk measurements.
6. Phase 6 shows everything clearly on the dashboard.


WHAT DOES THE DASHBOARD SHOW?

OVERVIEW
- portfolio growth chart
- drawdown chart
- final portfolio value
- total return
- annualized return
- volatility
- Sharpe ratio
- maximum drawdown
- win rate

TRADES
- every simulated buy and sell
- date, stock, quantity, price, value, and fee

STRATEGY COMPARISON
- moving average
- mean reversion
- momentum
- equal-weight benchmark

The comparison starts every strategy at 100 so their growth can be compared
fairly on the same chart.

DATA AND SIGNALS
- historical closing prices
- target weights created by the strategy


WHY IS DEMO DATA INCLUDED?

Demo data lets the dashboard work without internet.
It is always created in the same way, so tests and examples are repeatable.
It is for learning, not for making investment decisions.


IMPORTANT CORRECTNESS RULE

The dashboard does not perform trades itself.

It sends the strategy signals to the existing backtester. The backtester delays
each signal and trades at the next opening price. This helps prevent look-ahead
bias.

The dashboard also includes the selected fees and slippage.
